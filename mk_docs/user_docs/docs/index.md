## 👋 What is Digital Logbook?

Digital Logbook is a simple online tool that helps teams work together. Here is what each person does:

| 👤 Role | 🎯 What they do |
|---|---|
| **Super Admin** | Invites users, creates projects, sees reports |
| **Mentor** | Reviews student work and gives helpful feedback |
| **Student** | Logs daily work activities and reads feedback |

## What is Digital Logbook?

Digital Logbook is a centralized web platform that streamlines the full project lifecycle — from inviting users and assigning projects to tracking daily work logs and generating progress reports. Every role has a dedicated workspace tailored to their responsibilities.

<div class="feature-grid" markdown>
<div class="feature-item" markdown>
<div class="feature-item-icon">⚡</div>
<div class="feature-item-text" markdown>
**Fast onboarding**
<span>Invite users and activate accounts in minutes</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="feature-item" markdown>
<div class="feature-item-icon">🔐</div>
<div class="feature-item-text" markdown>
**Role-based access**
<span>Clear permission boundaries for every user type</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="feature-item" markdown>
<div class="feature-item-icon">📈</div>
<div class="feature-item-text" markdown>
