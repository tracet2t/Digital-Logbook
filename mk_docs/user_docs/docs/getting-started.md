# Getting Started

This guide will help you activate your account and log in to the Digital Logbook platform for the first time.

---

## First Time Account Setup

!!! info "Before You Begin"
    You must receive an invitation email from a Super Admin before you can register. Contact your Super Admin if you have not received one.

### Step 1: Check Your Email

You will receive an invitation email from the Digital Logbook system. Open your inbox and look for the invitation message.

**Cannot find the email?**

- Check your **Spam** or **Junk** folder
- Ask the Super Admin to resend the invitation

---

### Step 2: Open the Invitation Link

Click the invitation link inside the email. This link opens the registration page and activates your account setup process.

!!! warning "Link Expiry"
    Invitation links expire after a set period. If your link is no longer working, contact the Super Admin and request a new invitation.

---

### Step 3: Complete Your Registration

Fill in your details on the registration form:

- **Full Name**
- **Email Address** (pre-filled)
- **Password** — choose a strong, memorable password

Click **Submit** to complete your registration.

---

### Step 4: Login to the System

Once registered, you can log in:

1. Open the login page
2. Enter your **email address**
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click **Login**

You will be redirected to your dashboard.

## Daily Login Process

For returning users, follow these steps every time you log in:

1. Open the login page
2. Enter your **email address**
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click **Login**
5. Wait for your dashboard to load

After logging in, use the **left sidebar menu** to navigate the system.

---

## Understanding the Dashboard

The dashboard is your main overview page. Depending on your role, it may show:

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Assigned Projects** | Projects you are linked to |
| **Recent Activities** | Latest activity log entries |
| **Progress Summary** | Overall project progress status |
| **Notifications** | System alerts and updates |

## Navigating the System

### Sidebar Menu

The left sidebar provides access to all system modules:

- **Dashboard** — Main overview
- **Users** — Manage or view users (Super Admin only)
- **Projects** — View and manage projects
- **Activity Logs** — Log and review daily activities
- **Reports** — Generate and export reports
- **Feedback** — View and write feedback

Available menu items depend on your assigned role.

### Search and Filters

Most listing pages include a search bar and filter options to help you quickly find:

- Projects
- Students
- Activity logs
- Reports

### Pagination

When a page contains many records, they are split into multiple pages. Use the **pagination controls** at the bottom of the page to navigate between pages.
