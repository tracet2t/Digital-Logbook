## ✏️ Creating Activity Logs

<div class="section-intro">
💡 Every day you need to write what you worked on. This is your activity log. It takes just a few minutes and helps your mentor see your progress.
</div>

Activity logs are the core of Digital Logbook. You should update your log **every day** or after completing significant work.

### Adding a New Activity Log

1. Open the **Activity** or **Logbook** section from the sidebar
2. Click **Add Activity**
3. Fill in the activity form with the following details:

| Field | What to Include |
|---|---|
| **Tasks Completed** | List what you worked on and finished |
| **Results Achieved** | Describe the outcome or output of your work |
| **Challenges or Blockers** | Note any problems or obstacles you faced |
| **Next Planned Actions** | State what you plan to work on next |

4. Click **Save** to submit your entry

!!! tip "Write Clearly"
    The more detail you include, the better feedback your mentor can give you. Avoid vague entries like "worked on project" — be specific about what you actually did.

---

## Example Activity Log Entry

**Tasks Completed:**
Completed the database schema design for the user authentication module. Created entity relationship diagrams.

**Results Achieved:**
Schema reviewed and approved by team lead. Ready for implementation phase.

**Challenges or Blockers:**
Uncertain about the best approach for storing session tokens securely. Need to research options.

**Next Planned Actions:**
Research session token storage methods. Begin implementation of the login API endpoint.

## 💬 Viewing Mentor Feedback

Your mentor may leave feedback and suggestions on your activity entries.

### How to View Feedback

1. Open the **Activity Logs** section
2. Select the activity entry that has a feedback notification
3. Read your mentor's comments in the **Feedback** section

    ![Task Details with Feedback (Mobile)](<assets/myproject/student tasks Details mobile.png>){ .ui-shot }

!!! important "Act on Feedback"
    Feedback is provided to help you improve. Review it carefully and apply the suggestions in your next activity entries.

---

## Responding to Feedback

Although you may not reply directly to feedback in all cases, you can demonstrate that you have acted on it by:

- Referencing the feedback in your next activity log entry
- Showing improvement in the areas your mentor highlighted
- Updating your planned actions based on mentor suggestions

## 📈 Tracking Your Progress

<div class="section-intro">
💡 You can see how consistently you are submitting work. The monthly calendar highlights the days you logged activity. Green is good!
</div>

Your project progress is updated based on your activity log submissions and mentor reviews.

To stay on track:

- Submit activity logs **consistently and on time**
- Keep your entries **accurate and detailed**
- Address blockers proactively — don't let the same blocker appear week after week
- Review your overall progress from the dashboard regularly

## Best Practices for Students

- Update your activity logs daily or at the end of each work session
- Be specific about what you worked on, what you achieved, and what blocked you
- Read all mentor feedback carefully before submitting your next log
- Reach out through the system if you need clarification or support
- Log out after using shared or public devices
