# Contact & Support

Need help with your Digital Logbook workspace? Use the options below.

---

## Support Channels

| Channel | Best For | Response Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Email Support** | Account access, invitations, technical issues | Within 24 hours |
| **Project Admin** | Role permissions, assignment changes | Same business day |
| **Mentor Contact** | Activity feedback clarifications | Within 1-2 days |

---

## Contact Details

- **Support Email**: support@digitallogbook.example.com
- **Operations**: ops@digitallogbook.example.com
- **Documentation Team**: docs@digitallogbook.example.com

---

## What to Include in a Support Request

For faster help, include:

1. Your role (**Super Admin**, **Mentor**, or **Student**)
2. The exact issue you faced
3. Steps you took before the issue happened
4. Any error message shown on screen
5. Screenshots if available

---

## Service Priorities

| Priority | Example Issue | Typical Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | Login blocked for many users | Immediate triage |
| **Medium** | Invitation not delivered | Same-day support |
| **Low** | UI text correction | Planned update cycle |

---

!!! note "Security Reminder"
    Never share your password in support emails. The support team will never ask for your password.
