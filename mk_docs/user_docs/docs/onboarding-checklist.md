# Onboarding Checklist

Use this checklist to go from invited user to productive daily usage.

---

## Day 0: Account Activation

- [ ] Invitation email received
- [ ] Invitation link opened
- [ ] Registration form submitted
- [ ] First login completed
- [ ] Profile details verified

!!! tip
    If the invitation link is expired, request a new one from your Super Admin.

---

## Day 1: Workspace Setup

- [ ] Confirm your role and permissions
- [ ] Review assigned projects
- [ ] Check dashboard widgets and notifications
- [ ] Open your role guide and read required sections

## Day 2-3: First Workflow Completed

- [ ] Super Admin sent at least one invitation and created one project
- [ ] Mentor reviewed at least one student activity and gave feedback
- [ ] Student submitted at least one full activity log

## Week 1: Quality and Consistency

- [ ] Activity logs are submitted consistently
- [ ] Feedback cycle is active between mentor and students
- [ ] Project assignment list is up to date
- [ ] Reports generated at least once

---

## Done Criteria

Your onboarding is complete when all of the following are true:

- You can log in without issues
- You understand your role responsibilities
- You completed one full role-specific workflow
- You can find help through [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) and [Contact & Support](contact.md)
