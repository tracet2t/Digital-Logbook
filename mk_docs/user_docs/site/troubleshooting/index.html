    Troubleshooting & FAQ¶

    This page covers the most common issues users encounter in Digital Logbook and how to resolve them.

    Account & Login Issues¶

    I Did Not Receive the Invitation Email¶

    Steps to resolve:

    1. Check your Spam or Junk folder
    2. Confirm your email address with the Super Admin
    3. Ask the Super Admin to resend the invitation

    Note

    Invitation emails are sent from the system automatically. If your email provider is blocking them, ask the Super Admin to check the sending domain.

    My Invitation Link Is Not Working¶

    Invitation links expire after a set period.

    Steps to resolve:

    1. Contact the Super Admin and explain that your link has expired
    2. Ask for a new invitation to be sent
    3. Open the new email and use the new link immediately

    Warning

    Do not share your invitation link with others. It is unique to your account registration.

    I Forgot My Password¶

    Steps to resolve:

    1. Open the login page
    2. Click Forgot Password (if available)
    3. Enter your email address
    4. Check your email for a password reset link
    5. Follow the instructions to set a new password

    If no reset option is available, contact the Super Admin for assistance.

    I Cannot Log In Even With the Correct Password¶

    Possible causes and solutions:

    Cause Solution
    Account not yet activated Complete registration via the invitation link
    Account has been deactivated Contact the Super Admin
    Browser cache issue Clear your browser cache and try again
    Caps Lock is on Check that Caps Lock is off when typing your password

    Navigation & Access Issues¶

    I Cannot See Expected Pages or Menus¶

    Some pages are only accessible to specific roles.

    Possible reasons:

    • Your role does not have permission to access that module
    • You have not been assigned to a project yet

    Steps to resolve:

    1. Confirm your role with the Super Admin
    2. Ask the Super Admin to assign you to the relevant project
    3. Log out and log back in to refresh your permissions

    A Page Is Not Loading or Showing an Error¶

    Steps to resolve:

    1. Refresh the page using Ctrl + R (Windows) or Cmd + R (Mac)
    2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
    3. Try a different browser
    4. Check your internet connection

    If the issue persists, contact the Super Admin with a description of the error.

    Data & Content Issues¶

    I Cannot Find My Data¶

    Steps to resolve:

    • Check if any filters are active and clear them
    • Clear the search field and search again
    • Check the pagination — your data may be on another page
    • Refresh the page

    My Activity Log Did Not Save¶

    Steps to resolve:

    1. Check your internet connection
    2. Do not navigate away before clicking Save
    3. Try submitting again
    4. If the issue continues, copy your content to a text file temporarily and contact the Super Admin

    I Cannot See My Mentor's Feedback¶

    Possible reasons:

    • Your mentor has not yet submitted feedback on that entry
    • The feedback notification may not have refreshed

    Steps to resolve:

    1. Refresh the page
    2. Open the specific activity log entry directly
    3. If feedback is still not visible, check with your mentor whether it was submitted

    Invitation & User Management Issues (Super Admins)¶

    A Bulk Upload Is Showing Errors¶

    Steps to resolve:

    1. Download the error report or review the validation results
    2. Identify the rows with errors (e.g. invalid email format, missing role)
    3. Correct those specific rows in the spreadsheet
    4. Re-upload the corrected file

    Tip

    Rows without errors are processed successfully even if other rows fail. You only need to re-upload the rows that had issues.

    An Invitation Is Stuck in Pending Status¶

    The user has not yet completed registration.

    Steps to resolve:

    1. Confirm that the user received the invitation email
    2. Ask them to check their Spam folder
    3. If needed, resend or cancel the invitation and send a new one

    General FAQs¶

    Q: Can I change my email address? Contact the Super Admin to update your account email address.

    Q: Can I use Digital Logbook on a mobile device? Yes. The system is accessible from any device with a modern web browser.

    Q: How often should students update their activity logs? Students should aim to update their logs daily or at the end of each work session.

    Q: Can a student be assigned to more than one project? Yes. Super Admins can assign students to multiple projects if needed.

    Q: What happens if I accidentally delete an entry? Contact the Super Admin immediately. Recovery options depend on system configuration.

    Still Need Help?

    If none of the above solutions resolve your issue, contact your Super Admin with:

    • A description of the problem
    • The steps you took before the issue occurred
    • Any error messages you saw
