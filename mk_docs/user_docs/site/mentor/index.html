    Mentor Guide¶

    As a Mentor, your role is to guide and support students throughout their projects. This guide covers how to view students, review their work, and provide meaningful feedback.

    Overview¶

    Capability Description
    View Students See all students assigned to your projects
    Review Activities Read submitted activity logs
    Provide Feedback Write comments and suggestions on student entries
    Monitor Progress Track student consistency and overall progress

    Interface Preview¶

    Typical screen areas for Mentor users:

    • Student and activity overview section
    • Activity detail panel for context
    • Feedback panel for actionable comments

    Viewing Your Assigned Students¶

    1. Log in to the system
    2. Open the Students section from the sidebar
    3. Review the list of students assigned to your projects

    Tip

    Use the search bar or filters if you have a large number of students assigned.

    Reviewing Student Activities¶

    Student activity logs show what work was completed, what challenges were faced, and what is planned next.

    Steps to Review an Activity¶

    1. Open a student's profile or go to the Activity Logs section
    2. Select a specific activity entry to view the full details
    3. Review the following fields:
      • Tasks completed
      • Results achieved
      • Blockers or challenges
      • Planned next steps

    Consistency Matters

    Check whether the student is submitting logs regularly. Inconsistent updates may signal that a student needs additional support.

    Providing Feedback¶

    Feedback helps students improve their work and stay on track.

    Steps to Leave Feedback¶

    1. Open a student's activity entry
    2. Scroll to the Feedback section
    3. Write your comments in the feedback field
    4. Click Save to submit your feedback

    The student will be notified and can view your comments on their next login.

    Writing Effective Feedback¶

    Good feedback should be:

    Quality Description
    Clear Easy to understand with no ambiguity
    Constructive Focused on improvement, not just criticism
    Specific Reference specific tasks or issues
    Actionable Give the student something concrete to act on
    Encouraging Acknowledge good work alongside areas for improvement

    Example of Good Feedback¶

    "Your task breakdown is detailed and well structured. For next week, try to include the specific outcomes or results for each task rather than just listing what was done. This will help show the impact of your work more clearly."

    Example of Poor Feedback¶

    "Good job. Keep it up."

    Monitoring Student Progress¶

    Regularly reviewing student progress helps you identify students who may be struggling early.

    Signs a student may need extra support:

    • Missing or infrequent activity log updates
    • Consistently reporting the same blockers
    • Vague or incomplete entries
    • No improvement after previous feedback

    Be Proactive

    Reach out to students who appear to be falling behind rather than waiting for them to ask for help.

    Best Practices for Mentors¶

    • Review student activity logs at least once a week
    • Respond to activity entries with timely, actionable feedback
    • Acknowledge progress and improvements, not just problems
    • Support students who are struggling with additional guidance
    • Log out after working on shared or public devices
